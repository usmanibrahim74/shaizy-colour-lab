What works for a visa consultancy

  • Lead with a deep, steady colour. Navy, deep teal or burgundy reads as official and trustworthy, like embassies, passports and banks. People are handing you passports and fees, so trust comes first.
  • Let the accent carry the promise. Sky blue means travel, gold means premium, green means approved.
  • Use the accent sparingly. Keep it to buttons, highlights and one stripe, about 10% of a layout.
  • Avoid red as the main colour. It reads as refusal or warning. Also skip neon and pastels, which feel unserious for legal paperwork.
